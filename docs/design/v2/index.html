3 · Moving on the line

Subtracting walks you left

On a number line, adding moves you to the right and subtracting moves you to the left. Start at 2 and try taking steps.

start
2

tap − five times and watch where you land

watch out

2 − 5 is not 3. The answer lands left of zero.

Worked example

Work out −3 + 5.

  1. Start at −3 on the line.
  2. Adding means move right. Take 5 steps right.
  3. You pass 0 after 3 steps, then 2 more: you land on 2.

Think first, then check

What is 2 − 5?
