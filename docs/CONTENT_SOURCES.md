# ZENITH content and data sources: ranked directory and licensing assessment

Prepared 2026-11-10. **Verified** means the term was read in a source retrieved during this research (linked). **Check before ingest** means it is the commonly published term but was not confirmed here; confirm on the source's own licence page before the first import. This is an engineering assessment, not legal advice; one email to the rights holder settles any doubt.

**Ground rules for every source**
1. Free to read is not free to copy. Import only what the licence allows, and record the licence per asset, not per site.
2. Prefer official APIs and bulk downloads. Never bypass robots.txt, rate limits or paywalls. No scraping of sites whose terms forbid it.
3. Lessons stay original. Open sources inform facts, data, images and simulations; explanations are written by our authors and reviewed by a named teacher.
4. Every imported asset carries: source, URL, licence id, author/attribution text, retrieval date, content hash, and a reviewer. Missing any field blocks publication.
5. Share-alike (SA) licences are accepted only for assets shown as separate, attributed items. Mixing SA text into our own lesson text would force our text to be SA, so SA material is never merged into lesson prose.
6. NC (non-commercial) licences are excluded. ZENITH may charge schools or be funded commercially later, and the safe choice is to exclude NC now.

## What this means for ZENITH
- The curriculum **structure** comes from a human reviewer reading NCERT, not from copying it.
- Lesson **text** is original. Open sources supply **images, datasets, maps and simulation ideas** with recorded credit.
- The strongest immediately usable assets are: our own simulations (no licence risk), Natural Earth maps, Wikimedia Commons images with CC0, public domain or CC BY, World Bank and data.gov.in datasets, OpenStax figures with credit, NASA images with credit, and PhET CC BY sim files for reference or credited embedding.
- Anything under DIKSHA or NROER is a hand-picked, per-item decision, never a bulk import.
